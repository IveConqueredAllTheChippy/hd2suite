Unofficial, fan-made tool. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Arrowhead Game Studios or
Sony Interactive Entertainment. HELLDIVERS is their trademark.

Pictures (hd2suite\images)
  Made from the game's own files: armor and helmet renders, cape composites, weapon / stratagem / perk / booster /
  title icons, player cards, emote thumbnails. They are Arrowhead Game Studios' artwork and remain theirs.
  They are here only so that the editor can show what an item is.

Tools and sources this was built with (none of their code or files is included in this folder)
  Filediver, by xypwn and contributors - https://github.com/xypwn/filediver
      A modified copy was used to render the armor pictures and extract the icons.
  SHODAN's Stat Editor
      Reference for how the game's settings tables are read. Its icon pack was not used.
  The HD2 modding community's "LUT & Cape Modding Guide"
      How cape colours are stored.
  Helldivers Wiki
      Names of titles, boosters and perks (names only, no images).

Item names (part of hd2suite\data\items.json)
  Some names come from the community data set helldivers-2/json - https://github.com/helldivers-2/json
